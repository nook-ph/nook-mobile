# Placeholder cafe photos

Shown, labelled "Sample photo", where a cafe has no photo of its own. Generic
cafe scenes: no logos, shop names or faces, and none of them is a Cebu cafe.

All are from Unsplash under the [Unsplash License](https://unsplash.com/license)
(free to use, including in apps; no attribution required, given here anyway).
Cropped to 4:3 and re-encoded as WebP: `<name>.webp` is 1200x900,
`<name>_thumb.webp` is 480x360.

The order matters: the apps pick a photo by hashing the cafe's name into this
list (`CafePhotoPlaceholder.photos` in nook-mobile,
`PLACEHOLDER_PHOTOS` in nook-webapp `lib/utils/photo-placeholder.ts`). Keep both
lists and both copies of these files in step.
